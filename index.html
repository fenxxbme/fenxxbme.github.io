A little about me

Still learning.

I like figuring out how things work, where they break, and what we can learn along the way.

I’m an Informatics undergraduate at the Technical University of Munich. Lately, I’m interested in AI security, adversarial machine learning, and digital privacy. I also care about women’s online safety and the ways technology can help — or cause harm.
